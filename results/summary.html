Delivery change is a last-minus-first timing difference, including chunk phase and queues; it is not pure clock drift. Accumulated drift is a time difference, not a sum of delays.

Sample timestamps are simulated; read times are measured. Clock mapping does not remove delivery delay. Short-run drift estimates include LSL clock-estimation noise. Marker response detection uses the known synthetic baseline.

Flag thresholds are configurable, not physical synchronization guarantees. A recording NPZ file is saved per case; earlier result folders are preserved outside the project.
